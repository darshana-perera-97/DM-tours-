Galle: Colonial Charm Meets Coastal Luxury

Inside Galle Fort, centuries-old ramparts frame ultra-modern design suites and rooftop infinity pools. DM Tours combines heritage walks with spa reservations and private jewelry showings inside famed boutiques.

  • Stay in restored mansions with plunge pools and personal butlers.
  • Pair cycling tours through the Fort with curated antique shopping.
  • Enjoy sunset degustation dinners prepared by award-winning chefs.

Unawatuna: Crystal Waters & Premium Beach Bliss

A crescent of calm turquoise water sets the stage for lazy mornings and lively nights. DM Tours arranges floating breakfasts, reef-guided snorkeling, and discreet security for celebrity travelers.

Signature Moments
  • Champagne sundowners on a secluded sandbank.
  • Night-time turtle watching with marine biologists.
  • DJ-hosted beach lounges curated for private groups.
Traveler Tips
  • Visit Jungle Beach early for the calmest snorkeling.
  • Book couple's spa rituals after sunset boat rides.
  • Ask DM Tours to arrange a curated bar hop along the main strip.
