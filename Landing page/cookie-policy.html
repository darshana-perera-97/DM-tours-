1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and to provide information to website owners. This Cookie Policy explains how DM Tours Sri Lanka uses cookies and similar technologies on our website.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for several purposes to enhance your browsing experience and improve our services:

2.1 Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. They enable basic functions like page navigation, access to secure areas, and form submissions.

  • Session cookies: Remember your actions during a browsing session
  • Security cookies: Protect against security threats
  • Load balancing cookies: Distribute website traffic efficiently

2.2 Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting and reporting information anonymously.

  • Google Analytics: Tracks website usage and performance
  • Page views: Counts how many times pages are visited
  • User behavior: Analyzes how users navigate our site
  • Traffic sources: Identifies where visitors come from

2.3 Functional Cookies

These cookies enable enhanced functionality and personalization, such as remembering your preferences and settings.

  • Language preferences: Remember your preferred language
  • Currency settings: Store your preferred currency
  • Form data: Remember information you've entered
  • User preferences: Store your customization choices

2.4 Marketing Cookies

These cookies are used to track visitors across websites and deliver relevant advertisements.

  • Advertising networks: Show relevant ads based on your interests
  • Social media integration: Enable sharing on social platforms
  • Retargeting: Show ads to previous website visitors
  • Conversion tracking: Measure the effectiveness of our marketing

3. Third-Party Cookies

We may use third-party services that set their own cookies on your device. These include:

  • Google Analytics: Website analytics and performance tracking
  • Google Ads: Advertising and conversion tracking
  • Facebook Pixel: Social media advertising and analytics
  • YouTube: Video content embedding and analytics
  • Payment processors: Secure payment processing

4. Cookie Duration

4.1 Session Cookies

These cookies are temporary and are deleted when you close your browser. They are used to maintain your session while browsing our website.

4.2 Persistent Cookies

These cookies remain on your device for a specified period or until you delete them. They remember your preferences and settings for future visits.

  • Short-term: 1-30 days
  • Medium-term: 1-12 months
  • Long-term: 1-2 years

5. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

5.1 Browser Settings

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View and delete cookies
  • Block cookies from specific websites
  • Block third-party cookies
  • Clear all cookies when you close your browser
  • Receive notifications when cookies are set

5.2 Browser-Specific Instructions

  • Chrome: Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Cookies and website data
  • Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions > Cookies and site data

5.3 Opt-Out Tools

You can also opt out of certain cookies using these tools:

  • Google Analytics: Google Analytics Opt-out
  • Google Ads: Google Ads Settings
  • Facebook: Facebook Ad Preferences

6. Impact of Disabling Cookies

If you choose to disable cookies, some features of our website may not work properly:

  • You may need to re-enter information more frequently
  • Some personalized features may not be available
  • Website performance may be affected
  • We may not be able to remember your preferences

7. Mobile Apps and Other Technologies

In addition to cookies, we may use other technologies such as:

  • Web beacons: Small images that track email opens and website visits
  • Local storage: Store data locally on your device
  • Device fingerprinting: Identify devices based on their characteristics
  • SDKs: Software development kits in mobile apps

8. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the updated policy on our website.
